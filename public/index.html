Safety & Compliance

Terms of Service & Disclaimer

1. Discord Terms of Service & Account Risk

Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines prohibit automated actions using personal user tokens. Using third-party tools to interact with Discord endpoints carries an inherent risk of temporary or permanent account restrictions, loss of rewards, or suspension.

2. Zero-Storage Architecture

Your token is held exclusively in volatile server RAM for this session. It is never written to disk, database, external servers, or browser cookies. Terminating the session instantly erases all credentials from memory.

3. Educational & Personal Responsibility

This utility is provided strictly for educational purposes and personal workflow evaluation. You use this software entirely at your own discretion and risk.

4. Non-Affiliation

Quest Completer is an independent project and is NOT affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Discord Inc.
